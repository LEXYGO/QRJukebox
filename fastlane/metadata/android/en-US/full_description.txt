<p><b>QR Jukebox</b> is a lightweight, generic offline audio player that bridges the gap between physical objects and digital music. By mapping QR codes to your local music library, it is the perfect companion for DIY board games.</p>
<p><b>Perfect for Music Trivia Games:</b> Designed with versatility in mind, QR Jukebox is highly compatible with popular music trivia games such as Hitster™. If you have a local collection of the songs from your favorite game, you can use QR Jukebox as a fast, privacy-friendly, and completely offline alternative to the official online player. It even supports original URL structures, making it easy to map your music library.</p>
<p><b>How it works:</b> QR Jukebox scans a QR code containing a URL and maps it to a local audio file by mirroring the URL's path structure in your local filesystem:</p>
<ul>
<li>The URL domain corresponds to the root folder.</li>
<li>All path segments except the last one are treated as subdirectories.</li>
<li>The last path segment represents the track ID (filename prefix).</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Key Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Offline First:</b> No internet connection required once your music is stored on your device.</li>
<li><b>Privacy Focused:</b> No tracking, no ads, and no cloud sync.</li>
<li><b>Metadata &amp; Artwork:</b> Displays title, artist, album, and fullscreen cover art from ID3 tags.</li>
<li><b>Customizable Playback:</b> Set a global start offset (e.g. 30 seconds) to automatically skip intros.</li>
<li><b>Game Timer:</b> Optional stopwatch to track thinking time per card or scan.</li>
<li><b>Easy Navigation:</b> Quick 10-second skip buttons and smooth scrolling for long titles.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Supported Formats:</b> MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC</p>
